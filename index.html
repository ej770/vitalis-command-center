Sources: HHS “COVID-19 Reported Patient Impact and Hospital Capacity by Facility” (healthdata.gov dataset anag-cw7u), CCN 220071, weekly 7-day averages, Sep 2020 – Apr 2024 (federal reporting ended May 2024). Quality and rating: CMS Care Compare provider data, latest periods 2023–2025. Units are derived: Adult Med/Surg = adult inpatient minus staffed adult ICU; Pediatric = all inpatient minus adult; Peds & Neonatal ICU = total ICU minus adult ICU. Room-level detail in the 3D view (room number, status mix, day of stay, clinical group) is illustrative, generated from the real unit totals; the source reports totals only. Levers and forecasts are projections. Building: departments are stacked on six illustrative levels (the real campus spans several buildings); Med/Surg is split across two floors. Nurse staffing: ratio targets from the Massachusetts ICU staffing law (2014) and California Title 22; RN schedules, shift fill rates, OR and PACU counts and surgical volumes are assumptions. ED pipeline: real weekly ED visits, census and staffed beds, and the CMS median ED stay; ED admit rate, ED treatment spaces, unit mix of ED admits, average length of stay and day-of-week profiles are stated assumptions. Independent analysis, not affiliated with the hospital.
